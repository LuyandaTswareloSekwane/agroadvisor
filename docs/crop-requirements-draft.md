### Limits that must be disclosed to farmers

- NASA POWER's meteorological grid resolution is 0.5° × 0.625°, roughly 50 km scale; it is not field-level measurement. See [NASA POWER data services](https://power.larc.nasa.gov/docs/services/).
- CHIRPS v3 gives rainfall only, not temperature; its daily products are derived from pentad/monthly rainfall. See [CHIRPS v3](https://www.chc.ucsb.edu/data/chirps3).
- Climate data alone do not account for irrigation access/scheduling, soil, or microclimate.
- For later design, frame results as **climate suitability**, not “what you should plant.” Each result must explain why, cite the climate and crop-requirement sources, disclose irrigation assumptions, and say when evidence is thin or a source does not cover the farm region.
