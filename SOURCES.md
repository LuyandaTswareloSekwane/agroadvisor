### Climate-data and suitability limits

- [NASA POWER services](https://power.larc.nasa.gov/docs/services/) reports meteorological grid resolution of 0.5° × 0.625°, roughly 50 km scale. These are gridded estimates, not farm or field measurements.
- [CHIRPS v3](https://www.chc.ucsb.edu/data/chirps3) provides gridded rainfall, not temperature. The source describes its 0.05° product and notes that its daily products are derived from pentad/monthly data.
- Climate data alone do not account for a farm's irrigation access or schedule, soil properties, or field-scale microclimate. These must be shown as limits; climate matching cannot be framed as “what you should plant.”
- Every future result must explain its match/mismatch, cite the climate and crop-requirement sources, disclose whether the crop requirement assumes irrigation, and clearly state when evidence is thin or the source does not cover the farm region.
